About LeviTV

LeviTV is your real-time view of Levi in Finnish Lapland. One screen brings together 11 live webcams from around Levi Fell (including an aurora camera), the weather now from the Finnish Meteorological Institute, a 5-day forecast, northern lights activity (live Kp index and forecast from NOAA) and today's sunrise, sunset and day length. It works on phones, computers and TVs — no app or login.

LeviTV näyttää Levin reaaliajassa: live-kamerat, Levin sää ja ennuste, revontuliennuste sekä auringonnousu ja -lasku. Mukana myös Ylläs, Rovaniemi ja Helsinki.

Frequently asked questions

What is LeviTV?

LeviTV (levitv.com) is a real-time view of Levi in Finnish Lapland: live webcams, the weather now and a 5-day forecast, northern lights activity and today's daylight. It also has live views of Ylläs, Rovaniemi and Helsinki.

Is LeviTV free?

Yes. It is free to watch on a phone, computer or TV and needs no app or login.

Can I show LeviTV on a TV or hotel lobby screen?

Yes. Open levitv.com/levi/full/ in the screen's browser for a full-screen 1080p view. Click or press F for full screen.

Where does the data come from?

Cameras are public YouTube live streams (Levi Ski Resort, Ylläs Ski Resort, City of Rovaniemi, Port of Helsinki and others), weather is from the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) and northern lights data from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center. Sunrise and sunset are calculated. LeviTV is an independent service, not an official Levi channel.

How can I advertise on LeviTV?

Contact hello@oomf.fi. Ads appear on LeviTV screens and on the mobile view, with QR codes that are tracked for scans.

On a TV or display: open the full-screen link and click or press F. Name the screen with ?screen=hotel-lobby at the end of the link.
