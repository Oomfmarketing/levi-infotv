Privacy Policy

LeviTV — levitv.com and the LeviTV app for Android TV · Last updated 9 October 2026

Who we are

LeviTV is provided by OOMF Marketing Oy, Finland. Contact: hello@oomf.fi.

What LeviTV collects

LeviTV has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or location. We use Umami, a cookieless analytics service, to count page views and a few anonymous events: which location and view is open, camera selections, ad clicks and QR code scans, and a periodic "screen online" signal from TV screens. Umami records technical details such as browser, device type and country derived from the IP address; it does not store IP addresses or set cookies, and the data cannot be used to identify you.

TV screens may be given a screen name (for example hotel-lobby) so that we can see which screens are online. The Android TV app creates a screen name from a shortened, random device identifier.

Third-party content

Live cameras are embedded YouTube videos. When a camera plays, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may set cookies or collect data under Google's privacy policy. Weather data is loaded from the Finnish Meteorological Institute and northern lights data from NOAA; these services receive your IP address when the data is requested. Ad QR codes and links take you to the advertiser's website, which has its own privacy policy.

The Android TV app

The app shows levitv.com full screen. It stores your settings (location, layout, start on boot, screen name) on the device only. It needs internet access and permission to start when the TV starts. It does not access your files, microphone, camera or contacts.

Retention and your rights

Anonymous analytics are kept for up to 12 months. Because we don't hold personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you can contact us at hello@oomf.fi with any question or request.
